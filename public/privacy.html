Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ViralContent Dashboard ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

ViralContent Dashboard is a personal management tool. The application stores data entirely locally in your browser's LocalStorage. We do not maintain external databases or servers that store your personal information.

When you connect third-party APIs (such as YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok), the application retrieves data directly from those services to display it on your dashboard. This includes your follower counts, engagement metrics, and recent posts.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information retrieved via APIs is used strictly to display analytics and metrics within your personal dashboard. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties. The data remains on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

Our application interacts with third-party APIs. By using these integrations, you are also bound by their respective Privacy Policies:

  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy
  • Meta / Instagram Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy

4. Data Security

Since data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on the security of your own device and browser. We recommend keeping your device secured with a password and not sharing your API keys.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date of this Privacy Policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the administrator of this dashboard instance.
